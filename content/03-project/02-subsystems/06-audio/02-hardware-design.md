# Hardware Design

The current hardware implementation of the Audio subsystem uses commercial off-the-shelf modules, namely two Adafruit MAX98357A Inter-IC Sound (I2S) Class-D amplifier breakout boards, two PUI Audio AS04004PR-R 4 Ω speakers, and a USB mini microphone mounted inside the 3D-printed body, without any custom PCB. Future iterations may integrate the bare MAX98357A amplifier ICs and a surface-mount Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS) microphone onto a custom PCB to save space and tighten the budget further.
